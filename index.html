01 — DOME FEATURES

Gradually straightens the lower part of the shell to a circular, vertical-sided perimeter.
Clusters lengths into the fewest groups within the specified per-member correction. Members are shortened/extended at both ends equally in the preview and exports. Joint fit may change.

02 — MATERIAL

Visually clips each timber against bisector planes between connected members. This creates angled or compound end cuts instead of shortening the complete prism. Manufacturing cut coordinates and solid exports are not yet supported.

03 — CONNECTIONS

Or click a hub point in the full dome. All tabs share a concentric through-bolt center at the selected hub. Their separate axial offsets no longer spread the holes apart. The tabs are schematic; physical stacking and bend geometry are not yet resolved.
Start offset is signed along the outward normal: negative moves the start inside the dome. Direction sets which way the cylinder extends from that point. Sketch preview only; actual connector STEP solids need a backend update.
Connectors use actual strut directions, including 4-, 5-, and 6-way hubs. Preview holes are markers. The included optional Python STEP converter creates fused hub solids with socket bores and drilled cross-holes; browser STL/OBJ export does not.
Straight and sleeved tubes stop short of the common hub to prevent overlap. Clearance is included in cut lengths.
Tabbed ends and multi-socket connectors are shown as 3D-projected preview geometry (tab bolt openings are concentric at each shared hub; physical tab bends, stacked plates, and bolt geometry are not modeled). Browser STL/OBJ exports contain struts only. The separate CadQuery STEP converter now generates hub solids with socket bores and drilled through-holes; connector mass is not included in the on-screen takeoff. Tab width automatically equals π × outside diameter / 2; end clip radius is adjustable. Check fabrication feasibility independently.

04 — ENTRANCE

Door bottom follows the flat-cut dome base; adjust angle, tangential slide, and radial clearance.
Door plane is vertical and faces radially outward. Negative radial offset moves it inward. Whole intersecting members are removed, not cut into new partial struts. Additional framing and load paths require independent design.

05 — ADDITIONAL STRUTS

Link any two dome hubs or doorway corners. In click-to-link mode choose a starting point, then click multiple destinations to make several struts from the same point. Use New start point to switch hubs. Drag to rotate; click without dragging to select.
No supplemental struts yet.
Supplemental members share the current stock profile and enter the cut list, 3D export and printable map. Door frame corners are reference positions only; door framing and fastening are not generated.

06 — COVERING

Enable covering to estimate surface area. This does not create panels or cutouts.

07 — EXPORT

STEP assembly (including drilled multi-socket hubs) is generated with the included CadQuery Python converter and exported project JSON. Browser STL/OBJ files still contain only struts. Native SLDASM is not generated.

3D preview works offline; drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.
